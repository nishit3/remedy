Order totals are occasionally off by a cent or two when a discount is
applied to an order with multiple line items.

Examples:
- 3 gizmos + 1 widget with a 33% discount should total $14.46, but the
  system calculates $14.45.
- 4 gizmos + 4 gadgets with a 37% discount should total $49.76, but the
  system calculates $49.74.

Single-item orders, and orders with no discount, calculate correctly --
this only shows up once there are multiple line items AND a discount
percentage involved together.

I haven't been able to pin down which part of the pricing pipeline is
responsible. An order total goes through a catalog lookup, per-line
pricing, discount application, and tax on top of that -- somewhere in
that chain the rounding is happening at the wrong point relative to the
discount.
